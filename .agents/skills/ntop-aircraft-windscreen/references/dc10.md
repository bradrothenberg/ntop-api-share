## R24 lower surround and bounded tip

The user accepted the eyebrow, requested a closer lower surround and nose, then rejected protruding sheets in the R24 preview. R24d corrected the tip support. The user later accepted its appearance and requested a render-only quality improvement. Keep these acceptance scopes separate.

### Precision and source shape

The complete wrapped lower panel is not a plane. Shell 2898 has a best-plane RMS residual of 87.573 mm across 6,084 vertices. This does not disprove a local planar region; classify the actual trimmed patch being reconstructed.

The expanded power-basis lower-panel fit had a measured float32 difference up to 2.70070 mm. A 96 by 96 local cubic coefficient table reproduced the original fit within 0.00615 mm. Its float32 check differed by at most 0.00608 mm. Eight native calibration points passed within 3.284e-8 m. These results support that local representation, not arbitrary table sizes or all rendering backends.

Evidence: `lower_precision_diagnostic_r24.json`, `lower_stable_table_r24.json`, and the `lower_stable_calibration_r24` probe artifacts in `demos/dc10/output/`. Builders are `diagnose_lower_precision.py`, `stable_lower_table_r24.py`, and `build_lower_revision_r24.py` under `demos/dc10/scripts/`.

### Failure: a tip fit escaped its transverse domain

R24b fitted the forward cap with an even-X polynomial of degree 10. Its axial support faded between Y=6.15 and 6.40 m, but transverse extrapolation remained active. That extrapolation created unwanted sheets. Repeating the same host rendering on R23c and disabling the R24 tip correction isolated the cause.

R24d uses q=(X/sx)^2+((Z-z_tip)/sz)^2. The measured scales are approximately sx=0.645865 m and sz=0.630173 m. A quintic transverse weight fades over q=0.5 to 1 and is zero beyond q=1. It multiplies the axial support. These fade thresholds are case-specific design choices checked by regression, not measured aircraft dimensions or a universal guarantee.

The measured source tip is approximately (0, 6.0706, -0.381) m. Do not infer the true tip from the first vertex of a different tessellation. The finer mesh did not include that same extremum.

### Render-only refinement

The first host preview combined thin glazing volumes on a coarse grid and classified colors within an 18 mm band. It showed sawtooth edges. Raising pixel resolution alone would retain those defects.

`render_r24_clean.py` instead renders the shared exterior zero surface and clips pane colors using recipe trim fields. The nominal front frame inset is 11 mm. It projects window-region vertices back onto the unchanged field. The 192-cubed extraction produced 249,580 triangles and a maximum projected field residual of 3.012e-7 m. Images were rendered at 2560 by 1440 pixels, then downsampled for the report. Model geometry did not change.

This is a host-rendered exterior approximation. Thin interior glass and frame thickness is omitted. The finite projection residual does not certify surface topology or native display behavior. The clean appearance was inspected, but no successful new native screenshot is claimed. Keep this caption distinction when reusing `cockpit_r24d_clean_192.png` or the report images.
