# F-16: recorded construction lessons

[R6 report](../../reports/F-16.html) contains the native views and measured section results.

- Fit a sparse cubic guide before adding control points. Dense interpolation can preserve small oscillations that appear as aft-fuselage ripples.
- Inspect upper and lower surfaces together with the retained chine boundaries. A smooth longitudinal curve does not prove a fair complete loft.
- Define a finite trailing edge in the section construction. R6 uses 12.7 mm full thickness at the 32 ft default span. Preserve the explicit scale conversion.
- Keep tail edge straightness and thickness as separate constraints. Inspect isolated tail and fin views in addition to the complete exterior.
- Retain fixed camera registration for silhouette comparisons. Plan, side, and front metrics test different projected boundaries.
- The Onshape and Fusion reports compare against nTop R3. Their feature-tree and solid-body results do not validate the later R6 geometry.

The public recipe replays the saved graph. It does not rerun historical drawing registration or fitting. New shape edits need new native measurements.
