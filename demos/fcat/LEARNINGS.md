# F-Cat: recorded geometry and aero lessons

[Geometry report](../../reports/F-Cat-Engineering.html) | [Aero report](../../reports/F-Cat-Aero.html)

- Build the horizontal span and canted fins as one continuous bent tail loft when a smooth shared junction is required. Separate fin unions can leave visible seams.
- Give the pod chine, upper wing fairing, and landing-gear blisters separate controls. Inspect the assembled field after each local blend.
- Compare real sections of raw and sharpened meshes. Sharpening can change topology or local edge quality even when the view looks smoother.
- The recorded mean drawing IoU is 72.55%, below the 85% target. Preserve the unequal top, side, and front results.
- Separate AVL induced-drag estimates, XFoil section polars, and viscous CFD. They use different models and cannot substitute for each other.
- Check surface pressure peaks, wall-resolution outliers, prism coverage, mesh refinement, and force convergence together. A residual history alone does not qualify a polar.
- Propulsion assumptions, payload, reserve, and lift-to-drag ratio control the conditional range calculation. The 1,000 nm target remains unproven.

The saved recipe replays geometry. Solver installations, raw meshes, run directories, and reference photographs are excluded. Report attachments preserve compact recorded measurements, not a runnable CFD environment.
