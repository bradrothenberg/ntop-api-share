# A-12: recorded construction lessons

[R33 report](../../reports/A-12.html) preserves the guide, section, cockpit, and silhouette evidence.

- Use independent upper and lower spline surfaces when one conic family cannot represent the section. Keep their boundary curves consistent.
- Inspect guide derivatives and native section contours. Finite section samples do not establish continuity over the entire surface.
- Separate cockpit crown shape from window borders. Planar pane construction and local corner fields give direct controls for the glazing geometry.
- Hold source registration fixed when comparing revisions. A better planform score can coexist with an unresolved front-view mismatch.
- Retain failed targets and source limitations. Historical public drawings and photographs are appearance references, not production loft definitions.
- Native material-side tests, section residuals, projected silhouette scores, and visual review answer different questions. Keep their receipts distinct.

Use the saved notebook as the named R33 snapshot. The recipe replay is a portable construction record. It does not rerun reference registration, parameter fitting, or native evaluation.
