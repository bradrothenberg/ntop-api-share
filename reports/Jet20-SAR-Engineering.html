Aircraft feasibility condition

At 110 kg and 20 lbf installed thrust, weight is 1078.73 N and thrust-to-weight is 0.0825. Steady, unaccelerated level flight requires lift-to-drag ratio at least 12.13 at this mass and flight point.

This balance leaves zero excess thrust at the threshold. Climb, banked search turns, acceleration and adverse installation effects require further evaluation. The actual aircraft lift-to-drag ratio remains unmeasured. NASA force-balance explanation.

Revised user target: L/D approximately 12

The revised mass reduces the required L/D from the prior 200 kg result of 22.05 to 12.13. This is close to the requested target. The engine requirement stays 20 lbf at 3048 m, Mach 0.5, ISA.

At exactly L/D 12, drag is 89.89 N, requiring 20.21 lbf. Available thrust has a 0.93 N deficit at the nominal 110 kg mass. The corresponding level-flight mass limit at 20 lbf and L/D 12 is 108.86 kg. These equality cases provide no climb or maneuver margin.

The prior reference-flow fuel allocation is unchanged: 58.72 kg at takeoff. It now consumes 53.4% of the aircraft mass budget, leaving 51.28 kg for all nonfuel items. Reducing aircraft mass does not by itself change the engine fuel-flow prediction.

08Sources and confidence

  • EOS_AL: EOS aluminium materials: AlF357 and AlSi10Mg. AM AlF357 is a beryllium-free derivative of A357. Machining and finishing are supported. Product properties do not qualify a Jet20 rotor.
  • CAST_NI: Doncasters Trucast: investment-cast turbine wheels. A producer reports turbine-wheel investment casting and 713C/713LC alloy capability. This is process availability, not a Jet20 supplier commitment or allowables.
  • H282: Haynes 282 alloy. High-temperature alloy with gas-turbine applications and AM powder availability. Wrought creep tables cannot be applied directly to a printed rotor.
  • H230: Haynes 230 alloy. Formable and weldable alloy used in combustion and other hot-gas-path components. Oxidation exposure limits are not structural allowables.
  • SS321: Outokumpu Core 321/4541. Stabilized stainless candidate. The producer distinguishes Core 321 from high-temperature-optimized 321H. Temperature and strength applicability must be checked.
  • PH174: ATI 17-4 technical data. Precipitation-hardening stainless candidate. Actual bar/shaft product data, condition, temperature range, fatigue, and toughness remain required.
  • SKF: SKF super-precision bearing catalogue. Bearing selection needs space, loads, speed, temperature, stiffness, vibration, contamination, and lubrication. No bearing is selected from bore size alone.
  • IN718: EOS IN718 process-specific material data. Reported coupon properties depend on the machine, process and condition. Do not transfer them to another process or infer turbine capability from room-temperature tensile values.

Material and route proposals are engineering inferences from these producer references and the current component functions. They are not supplier commitments, process approvals, or regulatory compliance claims.
