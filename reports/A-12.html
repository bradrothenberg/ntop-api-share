02Public reference and configuration

The model uses the operational single-seat A-12 configuration with the landing gear retracted. The CIA describes its shorter body, single cockpit, and less prominent chines relative to the SR-71. CIA configuration comparison.

The master span is 16.9418 m, converted from the Intrepid exhibit’s 55 ft 7 in. The traced length-to-span ratio gives a 30.338 m model length (derived). The source drawing controls proportions; it is not a dimensioned manufacturing drawing. Intrepid exhibit dimensions.

Original drawing limits

The original drawing is asymmetric. Symmetry limits a symmetric model to 98.46% IoU in its original top view and 93.06% in front view (derived upper bounds). The user selected an upper-half reflection for the new planform target. That symmetric target has no asymmetry limit.

The crown heights differ under the retained scales and vertical datums. A source-only nose alignment improves the local crown agreement but worsens the whole-front comparison. The original acceptance registration remains fixed. Scores apply to the retained raster trace. 03Guide lofts and blended shoulders

Longitudinal cubic spline guides control section width, crown, keel, and chine thickness. Separate upper and lower cubic spline surfaces form the continuous fuselage and chines. Native conics form the nacelles, inlet cones, and recesses. Native cubic spline airfoil sections form the wing and fins, following the F16 construction method.

Continuous transverse profiles create the curved body shoulder and thin chine shelf. The wing joins this shoulder through a smooth union. The nacelle has a broader outer transition and a tighter inner transition. Its inlet mouth remains circular; section shaping develops aft of the lip.

Section profiles from the related SR-71

The companion drawings by N. Okolelov show lettered SR-71 stations and transverse profiles. The common forebody envelope and the K-N body/wing section provide two shape priors. The higher SR-71B trainer cockpit is excluded. These published drawings supply no manufacturing tolerances. Section-source notes and limits.

The F16 lofting method moves these transverse control points along shared longitudinal guide fields. The two profile families change smoothly between x/span = 0.35 and 0.95. Separate width controls adapt the upper and lower profiles. No measured A-12 station-coordinate table was found. Native section comparison data.

All 22 cubic guides pass the analytic C2 knot check: position, slope, and second derivative agree across every internal knot. Native field evaluations independently check the spline implementation. C2 guide continuity does not certify the curvature of every combined surface.

The upper and lower nacelle conics use one transverse coordinate map. A larger inner blend rounds the wing junction. The recess follows the cowl crown and keel to retain its wall. The blend grows smoothly behind the circular inlet lip.

Guide continuity evidence · Native spline probes · Guide fit and source limits

Photo-fitted A-12 windscreen and straight facet borders

Public close-ups of A-12 60-6924 constrain the triangular front panes, center post, and quadrilateral side panes. Front and side faces meet along one shared frame edge. The four panes remain flat. The metal below and behind them now returns to the fuselage over wider regions. Continuous corner transitions replace the narrow ridges in R31. The front metal tip has a short straight bevel. A wider upper return joins the window facets tangentially to the roof. The narrow center-post return is removed. Compact C2 rounding smooths the mirrored roof intersection inside the opaque post. A shallow roof surface follows the source crown and limits the cockpit height. Frame widths and transition dimensions remain inferred.

Native facet-border checks: passed. 336 complete-aircraft probes cover the straight borders and normal offsets on both sides. Maximum on-edge field residual: 2.08e-17. Facet-border evidence.

Native corner-field check: passed. Gradient probes inspect both sides of the former lower/aft corner seam. The check uses 144 points in each model. It measures local field continuity and supplements the surface views. Corner-field comparison and limits.
