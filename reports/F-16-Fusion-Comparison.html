01Actual native models

The Fusion airframe is one connected solid. The canopy, hollow nozzle and two rails are separate solids. The model uses symmetric analytic airfoil sections and elliptical body sections.

Attachments use native solid unions. They retain some sharp transitions. Curvature continuity and local blend quality have not been measured.

Shape limitation

The Fusion model does not reach the nTop project's 90% drawing-overlap target. Its front-view wing position is too low relative to the comparator. Its wing-root cross-section is also simpler.

The Fusion length is 14.7548 m, derived from the USAF overall length/span ratio. Its nozzle ends at 14.2740 m. The retained drawing's nozzle station is 14.8716 m at this span (derived). These source conventions differ and shift the aft planform.

The drawings describe different F-16 configurations. Their disagreement prevents either silhouette fit from establishing production surface accuracy. No three-dimensional surface-distance comparison was performed.

04Red-team findings for nTop

Confirmed defects and coverage gaps
1. Component bounds miss edited material

A 25% wing-tip chord edit creates material beyond the fixed wing x bound. A 25% inlet-width edit creates material beyond the fixed cowl y bound. Native field probes confirm both cases.

Operations that depend on those bounds can miss geometry. A perturbed native render was not captured, so clipping in the rendered result remains unmeasured.

2. The retained reset test rebuilds a fresh recipe

The validation script deep-copies the original recipe for each case. Its reset result proves rebuild repeatability. It does not test recovery after edits in one open document.

3. Shape controls lack test coverage

The retained 120 checks are 40 probes across three builds. They test the default and uniform scaling. They do not sweep the non-scale controls or their combinations.

4. The accuracy claim has a limited scope

The retained images resolve 6.625 to 13.25 mm per pixel at the 32 ft span. They cannot resolve the nominal 2.926 mm edge allowance reliably. Duct centerline probes also do not establish minimum wall thickness or full passage clearance.

5. Default identity and silhouette scores hold

The delivered notebook, recipe, reference and native-image identities match the saved audit records. The recomputed nTop scores match exactly. The audit does not invalidate the saved default appearance result.
