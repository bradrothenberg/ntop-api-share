## Requirements, later nacelle, and inlet reviews

The [C5 requirements report](../reports/Jet20-Requirements.html), [engineering study](../reports/Jet20-SAR-Engineering.html), and [closure plan](../reports/Jet20-Closure-Plan.html) distinguish a sizing calculation from hardware verification.

[P4 nacelle](../reports/Jet20-P4-Nacelle.html) records added bellmouth and outer-panel geometry around retained engine parts. Original-mesh sections inspect passages and joints. Display offsets in an exploded view do not prove a feasible assembly sequence.

[P5 inlet](../reports/Jet20-P5-Inlet.html) is a bounded native bellmouth pilot plus proposed casing and joint sections. It is not a verified complete P5 engine assembly. Preserve that scope when reusing its figures.

Actual assembled and exploded nTop screenshots remain in the Jet20 demo and [P3 report](../reports/Jet20.html).
